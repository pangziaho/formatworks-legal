Privacy Policy

FormatWorks · Updated 2 October 2026

FormatWorks converts your files on your device. Your videos, audio, images and PDFs never leave your iPhone or iPad. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What the app accesses

  • Files you choose. When you add files from Photos, the Files app or another app, FormatWorks makes a copy inside the app so it can convert it. The copies and the converted files stay in the app’s own folder on your device (shown in the Files app under On My iPhone › FormatWorks).
  • Photos. The app only asks for permission to add to your photo library, so it can save converted videos and images there when you ask it to. Choosing photos uses the system picker, which gives the app only the items you select.
  • Nothing else. No location, contacts, camera or microphone. The app works without an internet connection and does not send your files or any information about you anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No data is collected, transmitted or stored on any server.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, email or password.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Purchases

FormatWorks is a paid download sold by Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, Apple ID or billing details.

Deleting your data

Delete files in the app’s Output Folder or in the Files app. Options → Clear removes imported copies and temporary files. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Open-source software

Conversions use FFmpeg and other open-source libraries that run entirely on your device. They do not send data anywhere.

Children

FormatWorks is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.

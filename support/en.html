Common questions

Where are my converted files?

Tap Output Folder on the toolbar. They are also in the Files app: On My iPhone › FormatWorks › FFOutput.

How do I save a video to Photos?

Turn on “Save to Photos when done” at the bottom of the main screen, or touch and hold a finished task and choose Save to Photos. Photos accepts MP4 and MOV videos and common image formats; other formats such as MKV, WebM or AVI stay in the Files app.

How do I make a ringtone?

Convert the song to M4R (up to 40 seconds). Then in the Files app, touch and hold the file → Share → GarageBand, and set it as a ringtone.

My iPhone video looks washed out after converting.

iPhone records HDR video. Keep “Convert HDR Video to SDR” turned on in Options (it is on by default) and convert again.

Can I use other apps while it converts?

On iOS 26 and later, conversions keep running after you switch to another app. On earlier versions iOS allows only a short time in the background, so keep FormatWorks open for long conversions; an interrupted task restarts automatically when you come back.

A conversion failed. What can I do?

Tap the task to see why it failed and read the log. Try another preset or codec in Output Settings. If it still fails, email us the log.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the privacy policy.
